# Distribution notice

This companion pack contains replacement artwork and build metadata only. It
must not contain the Automobili Lamborghini ROM, RT64 TMEM/RDRAM dumps, palette
dumps, or other extracted game data.

The pack targets the North American (USA) revision identified by the SHA-1 in
`pack.json`; the hash identifies the required input and is not the input itself.
Users provide their own legally acquired copy of the game.

Before publishing a release, replace the `null` license in `pack.json` with a
license that the artwork contributors have approved, complete `CREDITS.md`, set
`rights_confirmed` to `true`, and verify that any reused or AI-assisted artwork
has a distributable provenance. No release should be published while the
license is unresolved.
